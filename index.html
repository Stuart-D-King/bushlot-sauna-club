What sets us apart

Heritage. Craftsmanship. Mobility.

Heritage

Rooted in a Scandinavian sauna tradition carried across the Atlantic and passed down through three generations of one family. Every build begins with that inheritance.

Craftsmanship

Tongue-and-groove joinery, premium wood species, and close attention to every detail inside and out. You are acquiring a piece of functional art, not an appliance off a warehouse shelf.

Mobility

Built on a 5×8 utility trailer, your sauna goes where you go. Tow it to the lakeside, the campsite, or set it in the backyard. No permits, no fixed footprint.

The practice

Heat. Cold. Rest.

Three moves, repeated until you lose count. The sauna is only the first of them — which is why we build ours to travel to the water.

I

Heat

Water meets stone and the room changes. Fifteen minutes, maybe twenty. You talk, or you don't.

II

Cold

Straight out and into the creek. The shock is the point. Find your own plunge, wherever you tow it.

III

Rest

Sit. Let your body catch up. Then go again — because the round after the cold is always the best one.
