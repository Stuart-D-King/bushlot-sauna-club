The product

The Bushlot Sauna

A four-person mobile sauna on a 5×8 trailer — handcrafted, all-inclusive, and ready to go wherever the season calls.

Overview

Built to be used, not admired from a distance.

Each Bushlot sauna is a handcrafted, four-person cabin built on a 5×8 utility trailer. It tows behind a standard pickup or SUV, stores in a garage, and sets up anywhere you can reach by road — your backyard, a lakeside lot, a remote campsite.

The design is intentional and restrained. One specification, built the same way every time — a wood-burning stove, premium tongue-and-groove wood inside, engineered lap siding out. That is what keeps the product coherent, the brand honest, and every build a considered piece of work rather than a commodity.

Inside

Wood, stone, water, and fire.

Tongue-and-groove premium wood throughout, benches at two heights, and a wood-burning stove sized so the room heats up fast and holds.

Windows on all sides. Light moves through the room all afternoon, the woods stay in view from every seat, and the whole thing feels bright and open.

All-inclusive

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

Every Bushlot sauna is delivered complete and ready to use on day one. The purchase price covers all of it — no follow-up purchases, nothing sold separately that should have been standard.
