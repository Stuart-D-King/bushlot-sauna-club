Heritage & craft

The Story

Where a family tradition becomes a business, and a memory becomes something you can build.

Where it began

A grandfather, a sauna, and a lake in northern Ontario.

Stuart's grandfather was born in Austria and eventually found himself on the lakes of northern Ontario. His neighbours were Finnish and Swedish — Scandinavians who had carried their sauna culture across the Atlantic and shared it openly with the people around them.

He learned. He built his own sauna.

Stuart spent almost every summer at that lake, and the sauna was simply part of the rhythm of life. At the end of a long day working in the bush or out on the water, you went to the sauna. You sat in the heat. You talked, or you didn't. You stepped out into the crisp Superior air, jumped into the cold dark water, and came back in again.

It was simple, physical and honest, and it stayed with him long after the summers ended. The sauna was not a product. It was not a spa amenity or a wellness trend. It was a place where the body was cared for and the pace of everything slowed down.

The name

What “Bushlot” means.

A bushlot is a Canadian term for a wooded piece of land — the kind of rough, tree-covered lot where a family might clear a path to the water, put up a hunting cabin, and leave a sauna in the trees for anyone who wants it.

It speaks to something unpretentious and real. Not a spa. Not a resort. Your lot, your ritual, something you made with your hands on a piece of ground you call yours.

The name felt right.

The builders

Built in Asheville, by hand.

Stuart and his father are the brains and the brawn behind Bushlot Sauna Club. They designed and built the first sauna themselves, from materials selection through final fit-out, and found the work felt right in a way a lot of work doesn't. What you see in these photographs is that first build — the prototype, and the proof of concept.

Bushlot is based in Asheville, North Carolina, a place well suited to this kind of work. The culture here values what's local, what's handmade, and what lasts. That's the company it keeps.

Looking ahead

Built to grow, not to rush.

Bushlot is a start-up. Every sauna in year one is a proof of concept — a chance to refine the build, test the market, and earn the trust of the first customers. The goal isn't volume; it's quality that stands up over time.

Later offerings will include fixed-installation saunas for people who want a permanent home for theirs, and a small line of branded goods for the community that grows around it.

But that comes later. Right now there is one thing to do: build a great sauna for someone who wants one.

Our mission
To craft premium mobile saunas of enduring quality and artistic distinction, inspired by the timeless traditions of sauna culture — and to share the experience of heat, community and intentional rest with people who believe that how you live matters.
